# Saleem's Life Hacks

Last updated September 22, 2025

A collection of practical life hacks, tips, and strategies that make daily life more efficient, organized, and enjoyable. These are tried-and-tested approaches that have genuinely improved my quality of life.

---

## Daily Routines & Habits

### Morning Optimization

- **The 5-Minute Rule**: If a task takes less than 5 minutes, do it immediately rather than adding it to your todo list
- **Batch Similar Tasks**: Group similar activities together (all calls, all emails, all errands) to minimize context switching
- **Prepare the Night Before**: Set out clothes, prepare coffee, pack bags - reduces decision fatigue in the morning

### Evening Wind-Down

- **Digital Sunset**: Stop using screens 1 hour before intended sleep time
- **Tomorrow's Top 3**: Write down the 3 most important tasks for the next day before going to bed
- **5-Minute Tidy**: Spend 5 minutes returning items to their designated places

## Organization & Productivity

### Digital Organization

- **Two-Folder Email System**: Only keep "Action Required" and "Archive" folders - everything else gets deleted or archived immediately
- **Desktop Zero**: Keep your desktop completely empty - it's a workspace, not storage
- **Cloud Folder Structure**: Use consistent folder naming across all cloud services (OneDrive, Google Drive, etc.)

### Physical Organization

- **One-Touch Rule**: Every item should have a designated home where it can be put away in one motion
- **Visible Storage**: Store frequently used items where you can see them - "out of sight, out of mind" is real
- **15-Minute Daily Reset**: Spend 15 minutes each evening resetting your most-used spaces

### Time Management

- **Time Blocking**: Schedule specific time blocks for different types of work rather than keeping an endless todo list
- **The Two-Minute Rule**: If something takes less than 2 minutes, do it now
- **Energy Mapping**: Track when your energy is highest and schedule your most important work during those times

## Travel & Mobility

### Packing Strategies

- **Roll, Don't Fold**: Rolling clothes saves 30% more space and prevents wrinkles
- **Packing Cubes**: Use different colored cubes for different types of items (clothes, electronics, toiletries)
- **Essential Carry-On Kit**: Always pack a change of clothes, medications, and chargers in your carry-on

### Digital Nomad Tips

- **Portable Office Setup**: Invest in lightweight, multi-purpose gear that works anywhere
- **Local SIM Strategy**: Research local data plans before traveling - often cheaper than international roaming
- **Backup Everything**: Keep copies of important documents in cloud storage and physical backups

## Health & Wellness

### Nutrition Hacks

- **Meal Prep Containers**: Use glass containers that can go from fridge to microwave to dishwasher
- **Hydration Tracking**: Keep a large water bottle visible and aim to finish it twice daily
- **Snack Preparation**: Pre-portion healthy snacks on Sunday for the entire week

### Exercise Integration

- **Micro-Workouts**: 5-10 minute movement breaks are better than waiting for the "perfect" 60-minute window
- **Walking Meetings**: Take calls while walking when possible - improves creativity and health
- **Habit Stacking**: Attach new healthy habits to existing routines (e.g., do pushups while coffee brews)

## Financial Life Hacks

### Money Management

- **Automatic Everything**: Automate savings, bill payments, and investments to remove decision fatigue
- **The 24-Hour Rule**: Wait 24 hours before any non-essential purchase over $50
- **Subscription Audit**: Review and cancel unused subscriptions quarterly

### Smart Spending

- **Buy It For Life**: Invest in quality items you use daily (mattress, shoes, tools) rather than replacing cheap versions repeatedly
- **Opportunity Cost Thinking**: Calculate how many hours you need to work to afford something
- **Cash Back Optimization**: Use the right credit card for each category of spending

## Communication & Relationships

### Email Efficiency

- **Template Library**: Create templates for common responses and requests
- **Inbox Zero Method**: Process emails with DELETE, DELEGATE, RESPOND, DEFER, or DO
- **Unsubscribe Aggressively**: If you haven't opened emails from a sender in 30 days, unsubscribe

### Social Interactions

- **Name Repetition**: Repeat someone's name within the first few minutes of meeting them
- **Follow-Up System**: Send a quick follow-up message within 24 hours of meeting someone new
- **Calendar Blocking**: Block time for relationship maintenance (calling family, reaching out to friends)

## Technology Integration

### Automation

- **IFTTT/Zapier Rules**: Automate repetitive digital tasks (save Instagram photos to Google Photos, etc.)
- **Smart Home Basics**: Start with lights and thermostat - the highest impact, lowest complexity automation
- **Phone Shortcuts**: Create iOS/Android shortcuts for common multi-step tasks

### Digital Wellness

- **App Limits**: Set daily time limits on social media and entertainment apps
- **Notification Audit**: Turn off all non-essential notifications - check apps on your schedule, not theirs
- **Focus Modes**: Use Do Not Disturb modes for deep work, sleep, and family time

## Learning & Growth

### Skill Development

- **20-Minute Learning**: Dedicate 20 minutes daily to learning something new - compounds dramatically over time
- **Teaching Others**: Explain new concepts to someone else to solidify your understanding
- **Project-Based Learning**: Learn by doing real projects rather than just consuming tutorials

### Information Management

- **Capture System**: Have one trusted system for capturing ideas, tasks, and information
- **Weekly Reviews**: Spend 30 minutes weekly reviewing what you learned and what you want to focus on next
- **Curated Inputs**: Be selective about information sources - quality over quantity

---

## Problem-Solving Approaches

### Decision Making

- **10-10-10 Rule**: How will I feel about this decision in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years?
- **Reversible vs. Irreversible**: Make reversible decisions quickly, irreversible decisions slowly
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis**: Before starting something important, imagine it failed and work backwards to prevent those failure modes

### Stress Management

- **Breathing Techniques**: Learn 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8) for instant calm
- **Worst-Case Scenario Planning**: Often the anticipation is worse than the reality
- **Control Circles**: Focus energy only on things within your control

---

## Maintenance & Systems

### Regular Reviews

- **Monthly Systems Check**: Review what's working and what isn't in your systems
- **Quarterly Goal Review**: Adjust goals based on what you've learned about yourself
- **Annual Life Audit**: Evaluate major life areas and make strategic changes

### Continuous Improvement

- **One Percent Better**: Focus on small, sustainable improvements rather than dramatic changes
- **Feedback Loops**: Create systems that give you feedback on your progress
- **Experimentation Mindset**: Try new approaches for 30 days before deciding if they work for you

---

*This collection is continuously updated as I discover and test new approaches to life optimization. The key is not to implement everything at once, but to choose the hacks that address your specific pain points and build from there.*
